# formAPIS — Optical Mark Recognition Platform

> An optical mark recognition platform that reads answer sheets from ordinary scanner
> output and turns them into structured, scored results.

The full write-up is currently available in Turkish only. An English translation is in progress.

## Overview

formAPIS is an optical mark recognition (OMR) platform that works without dedicated OMR
hardware. It takes answer-sheet images produced by an ordinary scanner and turns them into
structured, scored results.

## My Role

Built by a three-person team. My responsibility was primarily **backend and image-processing**
work.
